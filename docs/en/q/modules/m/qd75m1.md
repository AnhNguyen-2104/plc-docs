## 1. Signal groups to wire

QD75M1 is a single-axis SSCNET positioning module. The signal groups to wire are:

| Group | Signal | Direction | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SSCNET cable | SSCNET optical cable | module ↔ servo amplifier | One fibre in, one out; daisy-chained through amplifiers (IB-0300062 §4.1.2 p.4-3) |
| External signals | 40-pin A6CON connector | driver/switch ↔ module | DOG, limits, emergency stop per the manual pin table (IB-0300062 §2.2 p.2-5) |
| Module supply | 5 VDC from base | base → module | Taken from the base 5 V rail |
| Ground | FG through base | — | Per panel rules |

## 2. Wiring principles

- **Connection type:** SSCNET connector — SSCNET optical cable to the servo amplifier (IB-0300062 §2.2, p. 2-5).
- SSCNET cable is a two-way optical cable; keep the bend radius and do not stretch the fibre.
- Switch power off before removing or inserting optical connectors; never look into a cable end while powered.
- Keep the connector cover on until insertion and refit it after removal.
- External signals go through the 40-pin A6CON connector; pin table and signal levels per IB-0300062 §2.2 and §4.1.2.
- Route external signal wiring away from servo power; use shielded cable grounded at one end.

## 3. Reference diagram

- Use [QD77MS4](/en/q/modules/m/qd77ms4) for a multi-axis SSCNET III/H line; QD75M1 is single-axis SSCNET, so only module → amplifier daisy-chaining applies.
- Use [QD75D4](/en/q/modules/m/qd75d4) for the 40-pin external connector layout and DOG/FLS/RLS/STOP group; its differential pulse pairs do not apply.
- Do not apply the [QD75P4](/en/q/modules/m/qd75p4) open-collector scheme.

## 5. External device connections

- Servo amplifier: MR-J2-B, MR-H-BN or a compatible SSCNET type; daisy-chain in axis order.
- Use the correct SSCNET cable; total line length ≤30 m (IB-0300062 §2.2 p.2-5).
- The last connector on the line must be the correct termination connector.
- Encoder feedback returns to the amplifier, not to the module; never wire an encoder directly to QD75M1.
- Never look into an optical cable end while powered.

## 6. Checklist before power-up

- [ ] Power was switched off before handling optical cable.
- [ ] Correct SSCNET cable and termination connector at the line end.
- [ ] Daisy chain module → amplifier 1 → … in axis order.
- [ ] Optical connectors are clean and covers are fitted when removed.
- [ ] Cable bend radius is within the manual limit.
- [ ] Total line length is ≤30 m.
- [ ] External signals match the 40-pin table.
- [ ] Module is in the correct slot and takes 5 V through the base.
