## 1. Signal groups to wire

QX70 is a 16-point 5/12 VDC input module. The module accepts either positive or negative common; choose one type for the whole point group. The signal groups to wire are:

| Group | Signal | Direction | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Input | X0…XF | sensor/switch → module | 16 points, 5/12 VDC; terminal table in SH-080042 §2-18 |
| Common | COM | — | Choose positive or negative COM and keep it for the group |
| Module supply | 5 VDC from base | base → module | Taken from the base 5 V rail; never connect an external supply to this rail |

## 2. Wiring principles

- **Connection type:** screw terminal block — 18 M3 screw points, one wire per point (SH-080042 §2.14, p. 2-18).
- Choose positive or negative COM before wiring; do not change type within one group.
- Route signal wiring at least 100 mm from power wiring; if they must share a bundle, use shielded cable and ground the shield on the PLC side (SH-080483 §4.8.2 p.111).
- Feed the sensor/switch from a supply within the module's 5/12 VDC rated range, on a separate branch and fuse; do not draw it from the base 5 V rail.
- Do not stuff multiple wires into one terminal; keep one wire per X point.

## 5. External device connections

- Either NPN or PNP sensors may be used, but choose one type and wire COM accordingly for the whole group.
- Do not mix NPN and PNP sensors on points that share one COM.
- Use shielded twisted pair for remote sensors; ground the shield at one end in the PLC panel.

## 6. Checklist before power-up

- [ ] Power was switched off before wiring.
- [ ] COM type chosen and kept for the group is correct.
- [ ] Sensor type is correct: NPN or PNP, one type only.
- [ ] Supply voltage is within the module rated range of 5/12 VDC.
- [ ] Every point was tested by hand before automatic operation.
- [ ] Signal wiring is at least 100 mm from power wiring.
- [ ] Shield grounded at one end, per panel rules.
- [ ] No multiple wires stuffed into one terminal.
