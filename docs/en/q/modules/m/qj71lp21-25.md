## 1. Signal groups to wire

QJ71LP21-25 is a 25 Mbps MELSECNET/H optical loop module. It has no electrical signal terminals; the groups to check are:

| Group | Signal | Direction | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Optical cable | Optical IN/OUT connectors | module ↔ next station | 25 Mbps loop; one fibre in and one out (SH-080049 §1.2) |
| Internal bus | Base connector | module ↔ base | Takes 5 V through the base; no manual wiring |
| Ground | FG through base | — | Per panel rules |

There are no pin numbers or electrical terminals for the optical line; the connector table and ratings are in SH-080049 §1.2.

## 2. Wiring principles

- **Connection type:** optical connector — two IN/OUT optical connectors for the MELSECNET/H loop (SH-080049 §4.2, p. 4-2).
- Use the correct two-way MELSECNET/H optical cable; one transmit and one receive fibre per station.
- Keep the cable bend radius within the manual limit; do not stretch or kink the fibre.
- Switch power off before removing or inserting optical connectors; never look into a cable end while powered.
- Keep the connector cover on until the moment of insertion and refit it after removal.
- The module takes 5 V through the base; do not wire a field supply into it.

## 3. Reference diagram

- No sibling page shares the optical interface. [QJ61BT11N](/en/q/modules/m/qj61bt11n) is an RS-485 bus; only line discipline and termination concepts can be compared — its copper cable, terminating resistors and DA/DB terminals do not apply.
- [QJ71C24N](/en/q/modules/m/qj71c24n) is RS-232/422 serial and does not apply to the optical loop.
- The loop diagram and ratings are in SH-080049 §1.2.

## 5. External device connections

- External devices are MELSECNET/H stations connected in an optical loop; each station uses one fibre in and one out.
- Do not fit electrical terminating resistors on the optical line; termination is defined by the optical connectors and loop configuration.
- Do not mix optical cable from another system into the same loop.
- Set station numbers and loop order in the network configuration before connecting cable.

## 6. Checklist before power-up

- [ ] Power was switched off before handling optical cable.
- [ ] Correct MELSECNET/H optical cable type.
- [ ] Each station has one fibre in and one out.
- [ ] Optical connectors are clean and covers are fitted when removed.
- [ ] Cable bend radius is within the manual limit.
- [ ] Never look into a cable end while powered.
- [ ] Module is in the correct slot and takes 5 V through the base.
- [ ] Station numbers and loop order match §1.2.
