## 1. Signal groups to wire

QY22 is a 16-point TRIAC module, 100–240 VAC. The TRIAC output switches an AC load. The signal groups to wire are:

| Group | Signal | Direction | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Output | Y0…YF | module → load | 16 TRIAC points, 100–240 VAC, 0.6 A/point, 4.8 A/common; terminal table in SH-080042 §3-5 |
| Common | COM | — | AC load-group COM |
| Load supply | 100–240 VAC | external supply → load | Separate load supply with a fuse |
| External protection | external snubber and fuse | — | Fitted outside the module, per load type |

## 2. Wiring principles

- **Connection type:** screw terminal block — 18 M3 screw points, one wire per point (SH-080042 §3.4, p. 3-6).
- AC loads of 100–240 VAC only.
- Route load wiring away from signal and other power wiring; do not bundle AC load wires with DC signal wires.
- Fit an external fuse in the load circuit; the module does not replace load protection.
- Do not stuff multiple wires into one terminal; keep one wire per Y point.

Use [QY10](/en/q/modules/m/qy10) to compare the AC load side and surge suppressor. The AC load and external protection apply; the relay contact does not — QY22 uses a TRIAC, circuit in SH-080042 §3-5.

## 5. External device connections

- Connect only 100–240 VAC loads; fit a snubber for inductive loads.
- Fit an external fuse for the load current.
- Check the OFF-state leakage current, which can hold a small load ON.

## 6. Checklist before power-up

- [ ] Power was switched off before wiring.
- [ ] AC load-group COM is correct.
- [ ] Load supply is correct: 100–240 VAC.
- [ ] Snubber and fuse fitted for the AC load.
- [ ] Per-point and per-common currents are within 0.6 A/point, 4.8 A/common.
- [ ] Every point was tested by hand before automatic operation.
- [ ] Load wiring is separated from signal and other power wiring.
- [ ] No multiple wires stuffed into one terminal.
