## 1. Signal groups to wire

Q64P is a power supply module for Q-series bases. The signal groups to wire are:

| Group | Signal | Direction | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Power input | L, N | supply → module | 100–120 / 200–240 VAC (SH-080483 §7.2.1 p.213–215) |
| Ground | LG, FG | — | Tie LG to FG and ground to ≤100 ohm (SH-080483 §4.8.1 p.110) |
| 5 V rail | Internal 5 VDC | module → base | Not field-wired; rating 8.5 A (SH-080483 §7.2.1) |
| 24 VDC output | 24 VDC | module → small load | No 24 V output |

## 2. Wiring principles

- **Connection type:** power terminals L/N/LG/FG — L, N, LG, FG and the input fuse (SH-080483 §7.1, p. 204).
- Fit a dedicated breaker/fuse on the input; do not share the branch with I/O loads.
- Tie LG and FG and ground them; LG carries half the input voltage and must not be left open (SH-080483 §4.8.1 p.110).
- The 5 V rail feeds the base and modules internally; never wire field signals to it.
- Add the 5 V current of the base, CPU and every module before choosing the supply; the total must stay below 8.5 A.
- Use power wiring up to 2 mm² and twist 100/200 VAC and 24 VDC pairs from the terminal (SH-080483 §4.8.1 p.110).

## 3. Reference diagram

- Use [Q61P](/en/q/modules/m/q61p) as the 100–240 VAC reference. L/N, LG/FG and the 5 V budget apply; the 6 A rating does not apply to other models.
- Q63P takes 24 VDC instead of AC; only LG/FG and the 5 V rail apply.
- Q62P adds a 0.6 A 24 VDC output; Q61P/Q63P/Q64P have none.
- Q64P differs only in the 8.5 A rating; its supply and grounding wiring matches Q61P.
- Supply selection and the current example are on [Power and base](/en/q/system/power-and-base).

## 5. External device connections

- AC/24 VDC supply through a dedicated breaker or fuse, with a isolation point for maintenance.
- Ground the panel at one point, ≤100 ohm; earth wire up to 2 mm².
- Connect 24 VDC loads only to Q62P, 0.6 A maximum; other models need an external 24 VDC supply.
- Never connect a load directly to the 5 V rail; all 5 V loads are modules on the base.

## 6. Checklist before power-up

- [ ] Power was switched off before wiring.
- [ ] Input voltage matches 100–120 / 200–240 VAC.
- [ ] Input breaker/fuse is fitted.
- [ ] LG and FG are grounded, ≤100 ohm.
- [ ] Rack 5 V total is below 8.5 A.
- [ ] Only Q62P feeds a 24 VDC load, at 0.6 A or less.
- [ ] No field wiring is connected to the 5 V rail.
